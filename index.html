Projects

Autonomous Quadcopter Redesign — CERLAB

Carnegie Mellon University · May 2026 — Present

Full mechanical redesign of an indoor autonomous drone platform in Autodesk Inventor. Identified and resolved two critical failure modes from the previous design: a rigid landing pad with no shock absorption, and adhesive-dependent arms prone to rotational failure. The new platform uses a carbon-fiber sandwich plate arm structure, a redesigned propeller guard that redirects impact into the CF arm, and TPU vibration isolation for sensitive electronics. Currently moving into fabrication and flight validation.
